HR.com Webcast Grabber

A one-click bookmark that reads an HR.com webcast page and copies its details so the Email Builder can fill in the fields for you.

1 · Install (one time)

📋 Grab Webcast

Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar. Don't click it here.

Can't see the bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl + Shift + B in Chrome or Edge.

2 · Use it

  1. Open the webcast page on hr.com (log in as usual).
  2. Click 📋 Grab Webcast on your bookmarks bar. A panel shows what was found, and the data is copied.
  3. In the Email Builder, click Paste from Webcast Page. The template type, title, date, sponsor logo, credit, hosts, learnings and description are filled in.
  4. Carry on with the Gemini step as before to write the subject lines and copy.

What it reads from the page

Template typeBreadcrumb, e.g. "Upcoming Webcasts / Standalone"
TitleThe page heading
Date & time"Date & Time" box
Sponsor logo & name"Presented by" logo
CreditCredit image in the description (e.g. "1 HR CREDIT"), then credit types
Key learnings"Here's What You'll Learn" bullets
Hosts"Your Hosts": photo, name, designation and company
DescriptionDescription tab, up to "By registering for this webcast…"
The AP shortcode and the Virtual Event name and link aren't on the webcast page. Take them from the flowchart sheet as usual.
