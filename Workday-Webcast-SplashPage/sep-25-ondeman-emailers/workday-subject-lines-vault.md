## 🎯 Copywriting Architecture & Rules Applied
- **Strict Mobile Optimization**: Every option is strictly kept between **42 and 54 characters** (under the 60-character mobile clipping threshold).
- **Zero Colons (:)**: No artificial headline stacking or robotic formatting.
- **Zero Prohibited Terms**: 100% compliant with On-Demand standards (no *"free"*, *"live"*, *"ondemand"*, *"on-demand"*, or dates).
- **Strict Question Rule**: Every question starts with an interrogative verb/adverb and ends with a definitive question mark (`?`).
- **High Cognitive Curiosity & Behavioral Urgency**: Structured across 6 distinct psychological buyer angles to maximize open rates across different audience segments.
