Executive Briefing

The System of Work: Why Workforce Management Drives Business Execution

View OnDemand →

Sapient Insights research reveals that organizations with advanced workforce management are twice as likely to achieve higher profits and customer growth. Yet, many organizations still treat workforce management as an administrative back-office cost center, missing critical opportunities to optimize operational agility.

Industry leaders Cristina Goldt of Workday and Stacey Harris of Sapient Insights examine how unifying HR, time, scheduling, and real-time labor data transforms frontline execution. Discover how emerging AI technologies and integrated systems empower leaders to maintain cost control while accelerating business growth.
