How can employers rein in rising healthcare costs without compromising the quality of care their people depend on? With projections pointing to another significant increase in employer healthcare spend for 2026, HR and benefits leaders are under more pressure than ever to make smart, data-driven decisions that truly deliver results.

In this on-demand webinar, experts from Truven by Merative share the latest healthcare cost trends backed by original research and insights from MarketScan®, one of the industry’s most comprehensive healthcare databases covering more than 300 million lives. The session explores what’s really driving costs — including the rapid growth of GLP-1 medications and gene therapies — and highlights practical, proven strategies organizations are using to manage spend while supporting the physical, mental, and financial well-being of their workforce.

Watch now to gain the clarity, analytics, and expert perspective needed to build a stronger benefits strategy for 2026 and beyond.
