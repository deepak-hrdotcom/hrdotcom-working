AI Orchestration:
How HR Leads Successful Enterprise Adoption

Join an elite group of HR executives for networking and this 90-minute, action-focused roundtable, brought to you by the Trusted Executive Network. We’ll break down exactly how HR and the C-suite can partner to drive successful AI adoption across the enterprise.

Most AI initiatives fail — not because the technology doesn’t work, but because organizations lack alignment, orchestration, and a clear human + AI operating model. HR is uniquely positioned to fix that. It’s time to make it happen.

What This Session Delivers

This is not another “what is AI?” conversation. You will walk out with 3 concrete actions your organization can execute within the next 30 days.

1

Build Your AI Orchestration Coalition (AIOC)

Learn how to align HR, IT, operations, legal, and business leaders around a shared AI “North Star” to eliminate the root causes of failed AI initiatives.

2

Map a Real Human + AI Workflow

Identify which tasks stay human, which tasks AI can support, and which tasks agents can automate — creating a clear Human + AI operating model you can take back to your organization.

3

Launch a Responsible AI Quick-Start Policy

Develop baseline guardrails — transparency, privacy, human review, and ethical guidelines — that enable safe, confident AI experimentation across your workforce.

Interactive Leadership Discussion

You’ll collaborate with other HR leaders to:

  • Identify friction points across functions
  • Design an aligned AI workflow
  • Draft practical governance guidelines
  • Produce one recommendation, one risk, and one next step per group

This session is built to create alignment, clarity, and momentum — the three pillars CHROs need to lead AI readiness at scale.
