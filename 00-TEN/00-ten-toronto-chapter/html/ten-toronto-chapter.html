How to Become a Toronto TEN Member

Step 1:

Fill out our brief application form, so we can verify you meet the member qualification standards.

Step 2:

The TEN chapter director will contact you with details.

Step 3:

You will be placed into a group of up to 16 peers.

 

Why Join Fellow Toronto-Area HR Leaders in TEN!

Enjoy Regular Meetings With Peers

Build valuable relationships and benefit from ongoing collaboration. 5 in-person, facilitated sessions (2–3 hrs) + 5 virtual check-ins + an annual retreat!

Gain New Perspective

Exchange insights with people who have the same HR challenges and opportunities. Share experiences and see what’s working — and not working — for others.

Find Support

Be part of an engaging and exclusive group that promotes each other’s success.

Enjoy Exclusive Opportunities & Insights

Access HR data, special interest groups, and leadership branding opportunities.
