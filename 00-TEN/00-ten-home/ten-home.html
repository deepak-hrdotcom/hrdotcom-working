What Is the Trusted Executive Network?

TEN is the confidential, exclusive peer network where you build trusting, mutually beneficial relationships with fellow senior HR leaders. Executive peer groups have gained immense popularity, and now TEN brings this powerful model to HR.

Discuss Shared Concerns

A dedicated network to explore challenges and opportunities with high-caliber peers.

Safe and Supportive

Ask questions and share professional and personal matters (such as ones you may not feel comfortable discussing in your organization) with peers who understand.

Exclusive Opportunities

Enjoy special events and access learning, networking, and branding opportunities tailored for senior HR leaders.

How TEN Works For You

Grow Relationships, Gain Insights and Build Your Brand.

Confidential Peer Groups

Join up to 16 like-minded executives* in:

  • 5 in-person facilitated meetings (2-3 hours) hosted by members.
  • 5 virtual meetups for monthly check-ins and collaborative problem-solving.
  • 1 exclusive executive retreat experience involving the full network.

* NOTE: Your peer group will be determined by your location and your title.

Peer-to-Peer Discussion Forums

Get answers and advice from your peers in real time.

Tailored Research & Benchmarking

Stay ahead with custom reports and data insights.

Special Interest Groups (SIGs)

Join one industry- or technology-focused group for targeted learning and networking.

Leadership Branding

Enhance your personal and company brand through speaking opportunities, articles, and events.

Strategic Impact

Help shape the Future of Work conference in your local community and serve on HR.com advisory boards.

Who Should Apply?

TEN is designed for senior HR leaders ready to connect, collaborate, and grow.

Key groups include:

  • CHROs (under and over 1000 employees)
  • Next-Generation CHROs
  • Talent Acquisition & Employer Branding Leaders
  • Diversity and Equity Leaders - DEI Leaders
  • VPs Of Payroll & Benefits
  • Workforce Planning & HR Analytics Leaders
