 HRRI Strategic Recommendations
 Based on our research, please consider the following suggestions:
 1. Build organizational understanding and strategy around AI use in PM.
 Half of organizations still donʼt plan to use AI in performance management at all, and another 26% are
 only exploring or piloting it. Develop a clear, deliberate plan for how AI supports, rather than replaces,
human judgment before adoption pressure outpaces strategy.
 2. Pair AI adoption with clear guardrails and accountability to address distrust.
 Accuracy and reliability concerns (34%) and data privacy or ethical concerns (32%) remain the top
barriers to AI adoption in PM, ahead of integration and training gaps. This is compounded by a trust
 gap: Nearly a quarter of respondents believe employees distrust AI, and 18% believe managers feel the
 same way. Left unaddressed, this distrust will slow adoption and undermine the accuracy and fairness
people expect from AI-assisted reviews.
 3. Stay current as AI use in PM accelerates.
 Uncertainty about how AI will be used has dropped sharply, from about a third of organizations last year
 to just 10% now, while use across writing reviews (59%), goal tracking (52%), and personalized feedback
(35%, nearly doubled) has risen quickly. Given how fast this is moving, keep monitoring developments
both inside and outside the PM function so your strategy doesnʼt fall behind actual practice.
