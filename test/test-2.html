Beyond fire and re-hire: building a sustainable workforce for the future

View OnDemand →

The traditional "fire and re-hire" cycle is no longer financially or culturally sustainable. As AI accelerates skill obsolescence, replacing talent instead of upskilling drains critical knowledge and weakens organizational trust.

Access this on-demand webcast to embrace continuous workforce evolution. Discover actionable frameworks to build long-term agility and resilience through proactive succession planning, internal mobility, and strategic reskilling.
