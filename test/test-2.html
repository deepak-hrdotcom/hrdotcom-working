Form I-9 Compliance: Meeting Audit Requirements With Confidence

Form I-9 is a universal requirement, yet its complex and evolving compliance landscape is widely misunderstood. This confusion exposes employers to stressful audits, severe financial penalties, and unique challenges when verifying an increasingly remote workforce.

Join this in-depth session to gain clarity on current enforcement trends and E-Verify regulations. Discover practical steps to become fully audit-ready while transforming a rigid legal process into a positive onboarding experience for employees.
