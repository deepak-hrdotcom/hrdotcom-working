AI Can Find the Risk. Who Owns It? Rethinking Compliance in Global EOR

October 21, 2026 at 3:00 PM - 3:55 PM ET  ·  Free to Attend

Register Free →
 

Global EOR providers look identical until complex terminations or payroll exceptions create severe compliance exposure. Without knowing who truly owns these risks, organizations face dangerous liabilities in unfamiliar jurisdictions.

This session reveals how to properly allocate global compliance accountability and leverage agentic AI to catch workforce risks early. Discover how to evaluate EOR contracts, track severance, and combine AI with expert validation.

What You'll Learn

Evaluate continuous severance tracking versus exit-only calculations.
Identify termination guarantees and provider liability assumption.
Validate worker classifications using intelligent AI compliance tools.
Ask essential questions during your next EOR review.
