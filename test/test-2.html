Stop Being Handed Decisions: Giving HR the Data to Shape Strategy

December 2, 2026 at 11:00 AM - 11:55 AM ET  ·  Free to Attend

Save My Free Spot →

Employees check out when they feel unknown, disconnected from the bottom line, and powerless to fix the obstacles blocking their work. While HR spots these issues early, they often lack the hard evidence required to shape strategic decisions, leaving them to react to leadership mandates rather than driving them.

This hands-on session provides simple, budget- tools to close these critical gaps. Discover how to track team energy, connect roles to strategy, resolve persistent blockers, and generate the exact data HR needs to influence executive decisions.

What You'll Learn

01
Run 15-minute energy conversations using the Working Genius model.
02
Build a line-of-sight map connecting roles to strategy.
03
Set up employee-led check-ins and effective blocker logs.
04
Identify four crucial numbers to shape leadership decisions.
