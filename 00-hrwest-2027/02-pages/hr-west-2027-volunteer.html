WHY VOLUNTEER AT HRWEST

The HRWest Volunteer Program is the premier pathway to experience the entire conference at zero ticket cost while expanding your professional network.

Free Conference Pass

HR professionals can enjoy the HRWest conference for free by joining our Volunteer Program. Volunteers receive a free conference pass for committing to a minimum of 8 hours in volunteer shifts during the conference.

Volunteer Roles & Duties

Volunteers play a vital role in creating a seamless, inspiring experience for attendees across every stage of the event.

Badge Checking & Check-In

Welcome attendees with a warm smile, check credentials, and provide orientation guides.

Room Monitoring & Intros

Assist session moderators, introduce speakers, and watch presentation stages live while on duty.

Roundtable Management

Facilitate smooth discussions and assist facilitators during interactive roundtable workshops.

Wayfinding & Directions

Guide attendees smoothly through tracks, conference halls, expo booths, and lunch areas.

✨ Special Privilege: You may attend any session on the day(s) you volunteer, and room monitors can watch all live presentations while on duty!
