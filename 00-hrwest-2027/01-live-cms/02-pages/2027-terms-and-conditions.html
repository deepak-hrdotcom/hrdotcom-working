In the event of such cancellation by HR.com please see “Terms of Payment and Cancellation Clause.”

Exhibitor Regulations

Exhibitors must comply with all rules listed below and the below rules will be designated as the Exhibitor Manual. The wording below may be amended by HR.com or the South San Francisco Conference Center in a reasonable time frame with notice to the exhibitor, including without limitation the following rules:

GENERAL RULES

  • Exhibitors are prohibited from using any outside contractors for freight management and must work with the company that is assigned by HR.com.
  • All exhibitors must comply with load-in and load-out times designated by HR.com. All other times must be approved by HR.com. If you need to depart from the show early please gain approval from HR.com before the start of the show.
  • All tabletops must remain in their placed Fire Marshal Approved locations. A tabletop that is moved without proper approval from HR.com/Venue will be seen as negligence on the exhibitor side and may be grounds for disqualification from the show.
  • No exhibit may block access to or interfere with a neighboring tabletop.
  • Exhibitors are welcome to display signs, giveaways, or other promotional materials within their designated tabletop areas only. This must not include food or alcohol of any kind. Do not add any promotional materials to registration, food or beverage stations or anywhere on the show floor without HR.com approval.
  • Giveaways that can be considered hazardous or injury-causing are prohibited. HR.com reserves the right to preview any and all giveaways at their discretion at any time. Items that are marked as hazardous or unfit need to be immediately removed from the show floor and are prohibited from being distributed at the show moving forward.
  • Property damage caused by an exhibitor will be paid for by that exhibitor. Do not paint, tape, hang, nail, screw, drill, or tack anything to the walls, columns, floor, ceiling of the building, or adjoining displays.

EXHIBIT SPACE

  • 6’ table with 2 chairs; exhibit width space is 8’ (one foot on either side of the table)
  • HR.com is not responsible for any items attended or unattended. There will be no floor security; it is therefore strongly recommended that any valuable items not be left unattended.
  • Tabletop assignments will be made on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Payment must be made on time or space may be subject to change at HR.com’s discretion.
  • HR.com reserves the right to alter an exhibitor’s assigned space if it is deemed necessary; before exercising its discretion, HR.com will consult with the exhibitor.

SIGNAGE

  • All signs, displays, and products in a designated exhibit area must be related to the exhibitor’s company.

CLEANLINESS

  • Exhibitors must maintain their exhibit and the space surrounding their exhibit to prevent injury or destruction of venue property.
  • All Exhibitors are required to clean their area upon departure. All large objects and excess trash left behind will result in a clean-up fee of $100, which will be invoiced by HR.com within 10 days post-show.

AUDIO VISUAL (A/V)

  • Excessive A/V attention-getting devices, inappropriate images or effects, and offensive odors are prohibited. This is at the discretion of the event organizers.
  • All A/V must be requested through the appointed A/V provider including the use of a power plug for electricity purposes; this may result in a charge that will be the exhibitor’s expense.

FOOD AND BEVERAGE (F&B)

  • Exhibitors may only provide very limited, pre-approved samples or sealed take-away items, and no outside food or beverages may be consumed inside the Conference Center—anything consumed on-site must be provided by the venue’s exclusive caterer.

SALES ON THE SHOW FLOOR

  • No sales of any kind may take place on the show floor. This will result in immediate eviction by HR.com. Future business may be negotiated and contracted, but no money may exchange hands at the show.

LIMITATION ON EVENT HOTEL ROOM DROPS

  • Exhibitors may not make any room drops or hotel registration desk drops at the host hotel to conference guests without written permission from HR.com.
  • Permission may be conditioned on payment of a fee at HR.com’s discretion if another exhibitor has not purchased the ability to provide a room drop or hotel registration drop.
Payment and Cancellation
  • Full payment is due upon receipt of the invoice.
  • All cancellation requests must be in writing.
    • 5 - 3 months out Exhibitor owes 50% of the total amount.
    • 3 months out and under, Exhibitor owes 100% of the total amount.
    • If cancellation is due to a pandemic, refer to “Force Majeure Clause.”
  • HR.com reserves the right to deny, cancel or resell the exhibit space and sponsorship (if applicable) without notice after the refund request has been confirmed.
  • If an Exhibitor has an unpaid account, overdue amount, or has not tried to make payment arrangements 60 days out, HR.com reserves the right to cancel and resell the exhibit space and sponsorship (if applicable) without notice.
  • Any unpaid balance will be sent through the collection process if necessary.
  • No leads or further marketing efforts will be made upon non-payment or cancellation.

If HR.com should be prevented from holding the event for any of the reasons listed above that are beyond HR.com’s control then HR.com has the right to cancel the event in its entirety or any part thereof, with no further liability to the exhibitor/sponsor.

  • If the show is canceled due to any of the above reasons HR.com will refund all money paid less any costs that HR.com has incurred during the pre-show process. Alternatively, HR.com will transfer the full investment to another HR.com product to be produced within six (6) months of the event dates.
  • If an exhibitor wishes to cancel due to any of the above reasons a refund less any costs that HR.com has incurred will be granted.
