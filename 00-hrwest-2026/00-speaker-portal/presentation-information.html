Presentation Information

Roundtable Discussion

As a roundtable discussion leader, you’ll guide an engaging and dynamic session designed to spark meaningful conversations. Over two separate 30-minute sessions, you’ll facilitate discussions with two distinct groups of attendees, allowing for organic interaction and the exchange of valuable ideas. With no formal presentations or AV components, your role is to bring your expertise and insights to the table, fostering an inclusive, conversational atmosphere.

Session Presentation

Sessions are scheduled for 30 minutes, including Q&A time. Multiple concurrent sessions on both days of the conference. Each session typically sees between 100-125 attendees. There will be five minutes between sessions to check AV and connect with your Session Host. 

Computer and AV Tech

The session rooms will not be equipped with a laptop, so please bring your own. We still ask that you send us a copy of your presentation by June 4. You never know when tech difficulties may arise, and we want to support you with both a Plan A and a Plan B.

Session Hosts

We will have a volunteer session host in each session room to introduce you before your presentation and provide assistance throughout your session. Kindly send us your preferred introduction, limited to 100 words by May 10 to your assigned Account Partner or Lynn. Feel free to have fun with this and share something that our audience would not discover on your LinkedIn profile
