Volunteer Opportunities at HRWest 2025

Enjoy a free conference pass!

Apply to Volunteer

HR professionals can enjoy the HRWest conference for free by joining our Volunteer Program. Volunteers receive a free conference pass for committing to a minimum of 8 hours in volunteer shifts during the course of the conference.

This is a great option if the registration fee is over your budget, if your company won’t support your attendance, or if you’d just like to play a role in making the conference a success. It’s also a great way to meet new HR friends.

You would be a part of a small group of committed volunteers that help the event planning team keep the conference running smoothly. Our aim is that volunteers sign up for 4- to 8-hour shifts.

Volunteers will help with badge checking, room monitoring, speaker introductions, providing direction, roundtable management, and being friendly faces around the event. You may attend any session on the day(s) you volunteer, and all room attendants can watch the sessions in the rooms they are monitoring.

Volunteers are required to attend an orientation session virtually prior to the conference.

If you are interested in volunteering but can’t commit at this time, please contact us to sign up as an alternate. You can be our first call if anyone is unable to fulfill their duties.

We hope you will decide to join us! If you would like to serve in a volunteer role at the conferences, please email hrwest@hr.com
