You should have received an email Thursday with “know before you go” information, and we encourage you to review that. Here are a few key reminders, plus the Day 1 schedule at a glance.

Event Address — The South San Francisco Conference Center, 255 S Airport Blvd. South San Francisco, CA 94080.
Badge Pickup — Registration is in the main lobby. You may pick up your badge at the following times:
• Tuesday, June 9th, 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM
• Wednesday, June 10th, 7:00 AM - 3:00 PM
Parking — Limited complimentary parking is available.
The South San Francisco Conference Center has a dedicated parking garage attached to the building, but it is not large. There are several other convenient options. The Conference Center, Park Pointe Hotel, and DoubleTree Hotel operate under an agreement to share their parking lots; therefore, event attendees may park automobiles in any of the three lots. In addition, an off-site lot, located across the street between the Travelodge and the Best Western Plus Grosvenor Hotel, is also available for parking.
Mobile Experience (Important) — Most attendees use the Mobile Experience app to navigate the conference. We recommend that you log in to HR.com and visit the app prior to showing up.

Laptops — We highly encourage you to bring a charged laptop to the conference to benefit from several hands-on workshops and to enjoy the fun “Let’s Build!” challenge where you use AI to create a real, working HR product (if you haven’t done so, please create a free Replit account).
