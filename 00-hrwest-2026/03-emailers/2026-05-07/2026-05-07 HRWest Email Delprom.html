Expectations are rising. Change is constant. And many of the decisions you’re making aren’t easy.

Discover Leadership That Actually Works Now

"Psychological Safety + Radical Clarity: The Leadership Combo That Speeds Up Execution"

Emma Lidgett, Global Head of Talent Development & Learning, Kainos

Work is accelerating — and "old school" leadership isn't keeping up. In this fast-paced, highly practical session, you'll learn how modern leaders create clarity without having all the answers. Learn five leadership micro-practices you can use immediately to build trust, strengthen communication, and help your team move faster.

Make Your Organization Resilient Amid Constant Change

"Leading Through Uncertainty: Building Organizational Resilience in Volatile Times"

Jonathan H. Westover, Associate Dean and Director of HR Academic Programs, Western Governors University

Join a collaborative roundtable led by a trusted HR voice, focused on how HR can keep their organizations steady amid rapid technological disruption, economic volatility, and evolving workforce expectations. You'll explore real-world strategies for maintaining trust, enabling agile decision-making, and helping leaders at every level adapt without losing momentum.

Navigate One of HR’s Most Complex Conversations

"The Elegant Simplicity of Diversity, Equity, Inclusion and Belonging"

Christina Herrmann, Global Chief People Officer

Experience a thought-provoking perspective on how DEI efforts can move forward in today's polarized environment. Get ready to explore some of DEI's failures and to explore practical ways to drive meaningful progress using a framework rooted in discipline, honesty, and courage.
