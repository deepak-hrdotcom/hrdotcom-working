The Great Wine Tour

Nobody wants to be the designated driver on a winery tour. Instead of worrying about arranging transportation, join a small-group tour that transports you to three different wineries. Along the way, indulge in wine tastings (tasting costs included in the experience fee) and a lunch stop in the historic Sonoma Village (cost of lunch not included). On the way back to your hotel, stop by the Golden Gate Bridge, one of the most iconic landmarks in the world.

Along the way, your guide will share interesting facts about the region, its history, and its culture. You’ll learn about the challenges and secrets of winemaking, the unique characteristics of the terroirs of Sonoma County, and the importance of sustainable agriculture.

Chinatown Culinary Walking Tour

Muir Woods Tour — The Coastal Redwoods

Explore the famous groves of the Northern California redwoods on this tour of Muir Woods. Departing from Fisherman’s Wharf in San Francisco, a local guide drives you over the scenic Golden Gate Bridge and into the moist air of Muir Woods National Monument. Crane your neck to admire these 1,000-year-old trees standing 260 feet (80 meters) tall. Afterward, enjoy a drive through the charming town of Sausalito before heading back to the city.

In recent years, Oakland has earned recognition as a travel destination, including being named the top North American city to visit. It features a wide variety of activities and attractions that you can enjoy, including:

Lake Merritt Walk

Enjoy a leisurely stroll around Lake Merritt, a picturesque urban lake located in the heart of Oakland. The 3.4-mile path offers beautiful views of the water, wildlife, and the city skyline. It's a perfect way to relax and soak in the natural beauty of the area.

Redwood Regional Park

Take a break from the city and immerse yourself in nature at Redwood Regional Park. This park offers several trails that wind through stunning redwood forests, providing a peaceful and serene environment for hiking, picnicking, and enjoying the great outdoors.

Jack London Square

Visit Jack London Square, a waterfront entertainment district named after the famous author who once lived in Oakland. Explore the area's shops, restaurants and farmers market. You can also enjoy views of the bay, rent a kayak or paddleboard, or simply relax by the waterfront.

Explore the Black Panther Party’s History in Oakland

Founded in 1966 in Oakland, the Black Panther Party expanded across the country and the world — with more than 40 chapters and branches, including in England, India, New Zealand, Israel, China, North Korea, Vietnam and Algeria. Visit 12 Oakland locations where the Black Panther Party made history.

Oakland Zoo

While the Oakland Zoo does require admission fees, it can be an excellent option for an affordable and family-friendly activity. Prices average $25.

Oakland Museum of California

Explore the museum's art, history, and natural science exhibits that showcase the rich cultural heritage of California. Prices average $20.

Oakland California Temple

Visitors can walk through the colorful gardens lined with towering palm trees, sit by the fountains and cascading waterfall, enjoy the beautiful view of the San Francisco Bay Area, and feel the peace of the grounds. A coveted location for photographers, the Oakland Temple is one of the best locations to enjoy a sunset over the bay.

Speaking of the bay, San Francisco is just 12 miles west and San Jose is only about 40 miles south, making it convenient to visit sites like the Golden Gate Bridge or the de Young Museum, or events like Golden State Warriors or San Jose Sharks games. Plus, vibrant and eclectic Berkeley is just minutes away.
If you'd like to grab lunch while at HRWest, there are many options within walking distance of the Oakland Marriott City Center. Some examples include:

Blue Bottle Coffee

(0.2 Miles) Located at 300 Webster St., Blue Bottle Coffee is a renowned coffee shop known for its high-quality coffee and pastries. They offer a selection of freshly brewed coffee and espresso-based beverages, as well as grab-and-go snacks like croissants and muffins.

Tay Ho Restaurant

(0.3 Miles) Located at 344 12th St., this restaurant specializes in authentic Vietnamese cuisine. They offer dishes such as pho, banh mi, and grilled meats. The restaurant has a casual atmosphere and is a popular choice for lunch.

Miss Ollie's

(0.3 miles) Located at 901 Washington St., Miss Ollie's is a Caribbean-inspired restaurant known for flavorful dishes. They offer a variety of Jamaican and Southern cuisine, including jerk chicken, oxtail stew and fried catfish.

Ike's Love & Sandwiches

(0.5 Miles) Located at 2204 Broadway, Ike's Love & Sandwiches is a sandwich shop known for its extensive menu of creative and flavorful sandwiches. They offer a wide variety of options, including vegetarian and vegan choices. Their sandwiches are made to order and can be quickly grabbed to enjoy on the go.

Belly

(0.6 Miles) Found at 1901 San Pablo Ave., Belly is a contemporary American restaurant with a focus on seasonal ingredients. Their menu features a range of dishes, including salads, sandwiches and creative entrees. They also have vegetarian and vegan options.

Hopscotch

(0.6 Miles) Situated at 1915 San Pablo Ave., Hopscotch is a stylish restaurant that blends American and Japanese influences. They offer a diverse menu that includes burgers, ramen, sushi and inventive cocktails. The restaurant has a sleek and modern ambiance.

Umami Mart

(2 Miles) Situated at 4027 Broadway, Umami Mart is a Japanese-inspired kitchen and bar. They serve a selection of small plates, including sushi, sashimi and izakaya-style dishes. It's a great option for those looking for a unique dining experience.

Urban Legend Cellars

Located at 621 4th St., Urban Legend Cellars offers wine tastings in a relaxed and urban setting. They specialize in small-batch, handcrafted wines made from locally sourced grapes. Visitors can enjoy a tasting flight and learn about the winemaking process.

Ale Industries

Found at 3096 East 10th St., Ale Industries is a local craft brewery that offers a diverse range of beers, including IPAs, stouts and sour ales. They have a taproom where visitors can sample their beers and learn about their brewing process. Ale Industries focuses on sustainability and uses organic ingredients whenever possible.

Faction Brewing

Found at 2501 Monarch St. in Alameda (just across the estuary from Oakland), Faction Brewing is a waterfront brewery known for its scenic views and excellent beers. They offer a wide range of craft beers, including IPAs, stouts and lagers. Visitors can enjoy their drinks while taking in the panoramic views of the San Francisco skyline.

Crooked City Cider

Situated at 206 Broadway, Crooked City Cider specializes in small-batch, handcrafted hard ciders made from locally sourced apples. They offer tastings of their ciders, which come in a variety of flavors and styles. It's a unique and refreshing option for those looking to explore the world of cider.
