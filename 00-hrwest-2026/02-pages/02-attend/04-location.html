We’re thrilled to bring the HRWest Conference back to the San Francisco Bay Area! This year, the event will take place at the South San Francisco Conference Center, with our official conference hotel located just steps away at the DoubleTree by Hilton San Francisco Airport North Bayfront. Together, they provide a convenient and comfortable setting for learning, networking, and connecting with your fellow HR professionals.

The South San Francisco Conference Center is a premier event venue offering modern meeting spaces, spacious networking areas, and easy access for attendees traveling from across the region. Located just minutes from San Francisco International Airport (SFO) and close to major highways, the venue is simple to reach whether you're flying in or driving in or taking other transportation (e.g. BART) from nearby cities.

Our conference hotel, the DoubleTree by Hilton San Francisco Airport North Bayfront, offers beautiful waterfront views of the San Francisco Bay and is located just a short walk from the conference center. Guests can enjoy comfortable accommodations, an on-site restaurant and bar, a fitness center, and convenient access to nearby walking paths along the bay.

Parking

Complimentary parking is available, but it is limited.

No validation or permit is required—attendees may park directly in any designated lot.

The Conference Center, Park Pointe Hotel, and DoubleTree Hotel operate under an agreement to share their parking lots; therefore, event attendees may park automobiles in any of the 3 lots. In addition, an off-site lot, located across the street between the Travelodge and the Best Western Plus Grosvenor Hotel, is also available for parking.

Due to the limited parking, the Conference Center strongly urges guests to take public transportation. The Conference Center has complimentary shuttle service (Monday through Friday during commute hours) to the South San Francisco BART Station, CalTrain Station, and the Ferry Terminal.

Additional venue information can be found on the South San Francisco Conference Center website.
