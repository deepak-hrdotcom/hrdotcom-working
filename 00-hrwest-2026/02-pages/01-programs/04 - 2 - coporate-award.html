The HR.com Awards Nomination & Voting Process

1. Nominate

To nominate a corporate program, submit your entry by the deadline (with all fields completed). You may nominate a client, a colleague, or anyone you know!

Deadline: 5 pm PT on January 10, 2025

2. Voting

Once a nominated program has been added to a company profile, anyone can vote for it. The highest-rated initiatives will be crowned category winners. Cast your vote now and play a part in recognizing outstanding corporate initiatives in HR!

Deadline: 5 pm PT on January 30, 2025

3. Awards

Award winners will be announced and honored during HRWest.

Date: March 12, 2025 at the Oakland Marriott
