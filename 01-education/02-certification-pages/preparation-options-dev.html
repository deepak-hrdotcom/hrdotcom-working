Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our preparation courseware, Pass Assurance Guarantee, and learning access.

What is the Pass Assurance Program?

Answer: The Pass Assurance Program is our promise to you that you will pass your exam with the help of one of HR.com’s prep courses. If you don’t pass your exam, we will give you a complete refund for the cost of your course or we will pay for you to take your exam again. The Pass Assurance Program consists of four requirements. Please check out the Pass Assurance Program page to view the requirements.

How do I access my course?

Answer: All of HR.com’s certification prep courses are hosted via our learning management system The Hive. You can access The Hive by clicking on the small waffle grid next to your profile button.

What happens if I miss a class?

Answer: All of HR.com’s certification prep course lessons are recorded. Archived class recordings will be available within 24-48 hours of your class. Please note that watching an archived class does not count towards your attendance requirement for the Pass Assurance Program.

What’s does the Prime HR bundle option give me?

Answer: You can choose to bundle your course with an Prime HR membership and immediately receive access to member pricing. The Prime HR memberships include a vast selection of HR tools and resources to help you do your day to day tasks. Check out our Prime comparison chart to see what’s included.

What’s included in the prep course?

Answer: All HR.com prep courses include HRCP’s comprehensive preparation courseware (textbooks, 100s of flashcards, 800+ practice tests, and 2-year access to HR.com’s learning platform). You will also gain access to weekly review sessions held every Sunday. 1 on 1 coaching sessions are available as well.

Here are the suggested exams for you:

aPHR

Focuses on the fundamentals of HR principles and practices, designed for practitioners entering the profession.

→ aPHR: 10-week live online classes → aPHR: Elearning course (self-paced) → Print & online edition

SHRM-CP

For individuals performing HR or HR-related duties or pursuing a career in Human Resources (basic working knowledge in HR is recommended).

→ HRCI & SHRM: 16-week live online classes → SHRM: 16-week live online classes → HRCI & SHRM: 8-week accelerated live online → HRCI & SHRM: Elearning course (self-paced) → PHR, SPHR, SHRM: Print & online edition

PHR

Focuses on the technical and operational aspects of HR practices, laws and regulations.

→ HRCI & SHRM: 16-week live online classes → SHRM: 16-week live online classes → HRCI & SHRM: 8-week accelerated live online → HRCI & SHRM: Elearning course (self-paced) → PHR, SPHR, SHRM: Print & online edition

SHRM-SCP

For individuals with a work history of at least three years performing strategic level HR or HR-related duties; or for SHRM-CP credential holders who have held the credential for at least three years and are working in, or are in the process of transitioning to, a strategic level role.

→ HRCI & SHRM: 16-week live online classes → SHRM: 16-week live online classes → HRCI & SHRM: 8-week accelerated live online → HRCI & SHRM: Elearning course (self-paced) → PHR, SPHR, SHRM: Print & online edition

SPHR

Focuses on a higher end of professional design and planning.

→ HRCI & SHRM: 16-week live online classes → SHRM: 16-week live online classes → HRCI & SHRM: 8-week accelerated live online → HRCI & SHRM: Elearning course (self-paced) → PHR, SPHR, SHRM: Print & online edition
