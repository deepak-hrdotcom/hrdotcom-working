HR Certification Institute (HRCI) credits on HR.com
HR Certification Institute's (www.HRCI.org) official seal confirms that HR.com meets the criteria for pre-approved recertification credit(s) for any of HRCI’s eight credentials, including SPHR and PHR.

  • 15 international HRCI credits
  • 45 general HRCI credits

  • 45 general HRCI credits

01

What is Recertification?

Attaining your HR certification is an exceptional accomplishment. However, as an HR leader, you are required to renew your certification every three years! You can do this by completing professional development activities and demonstrating professional achievement to receive recertification credits.
