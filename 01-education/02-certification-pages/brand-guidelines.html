World-Class Certification Learning Experience

This living document serves as the visual and interaction source of truth for redesigning HR.com's certification suite. It transforms existing course content into a credible, modern, high-converting learning experience benchmarking global executive platforms.

MANDATORY OPERATING DIRECTIVE: Content & Data Integrity (Non-Negotiable)

The AI acts strictly as Lead UX/UI Designer and Visual Architect. Content, certification facts, syllabus topics, and disclaimers already exist. Your job is NOT to rewrite or alter content.

Rule 01 — Zero Copy Alteration
Do Not Rewrite or Modify Copy
Approved headlines, descriptions, curriculum topics, and disclaimers must remain word-for-word intact. Never replace verified text with generic marketing filler.
Rule 02 — Zero Invented Stats
Never Invent Numbers or Metrics
Do not invent pass rates, student counts, salary leaps, or star ratings. Only display authentic, approved statistics provided in official source documents.
Rule 03 — Zero Fake Promises
No Fabricated Guarantees or Timers
Never make claims the product does not support. No fake countdown timers, artificial urgency, or exaggerated salary guarantees. Authenticity builds executive trust.
Sections 1–4 & 39

Design North Star & Brand Personality

A first-time visitor must immediately feel: "I can trust this platform to help me prepare seriously for my HR certification exam."

70% Professional

Executive Academic Authority

Strong typographic hierarchy, restrained whitespace, credible information architecture, and serious academic tone. Clean structured layouts replace casual marketing fluff.

20% Modern

Modern B2B Product Precision

Linear-style clarity, subtle 150–250ms transitions, structured cards, hairline borders, and responsive grid ergonomics without gimmicky visual noise.

10% Human

Instructor Proof & Empathy

Prominent verified instructor portraits, authentic alumni outcome stories, accessible contact channels, and empathetic guidance for adult exam takers.

How to Avoid the "Googlish / Generic AI" Look:

Avoid plain white voids with floating candy-blue pills, excessive rounded corners on every element, random purple gradients, or 3 identical empty feature boxes. Use structured cards, subtle hairlines (#E5E7EB), restrained 8px corners on controls, and authoritative typography.
