HR Certification Institute (HRCI) credits on HR.com

HR Certification Institute's (www.HRCI.org) official seal confirms that HR.com meets the criteria for pre-approved recertification credit(s) for any of HRCI’s eight credentials, including SPHR and PHR.
HRCI is our preferred partner and if you were with us live, we’ll email you the code and place it in your HR.com account. If you view the archived webcast, click the ‘receive credit’ box to add your code to your HR.com account.

Here is what you need to accumulate in order to recertify online:

PHR/PHRi:

  • 60 general HRCI credits

GPHR:

  • 15 international HRCI credits
  • 45 general HRCI credits

SPHR/SPHRi:

  • 15 business strategic HRCI credits
  • 45 general HRCI credits

aPHR:

  • 45 general HRCI credits

You can now take all 60 of the required HRCI credits as webcast credits (no eLearning or quizzes required)

Strategic Human Resource Management (SHRM) recertification on HR.com

Here is what you need to accumulate in order to recertify your SHRM-CP or SHRM-SCP:

  • 60 Professional Development Credits, every 3 years

HR.com is a preferred provider of SHRM and accepts webcast credits as a form of recertification.

  • Live webcast events on HR.com (instructor-led) - unlimited use towards recertification
  • On-demand webcast events on HR.com (self-paced) - unlimited use towards certification

Note: Candidates must manually enter their activities into their records.
