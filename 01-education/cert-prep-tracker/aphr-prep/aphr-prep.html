Why become certified?

Choosing to become a certified HR professional is your first step to becoming your best professional self. Studies have shown that most HR practitioners believe that their current knowledge will only be sufficient for the next 4-5 years. By choosing to become certified, you will always stay up-to-date with HR trends, new developments, best practices, and remain HR compliant.

Recent research reveals that certified HR professionals earn an average of $10,000 to $20,000 more than non-certified HRPs and certified HR professionals are 37% more likely to be promoted. Not only are certified HR professionals more knowledgeable but they also earn a higher salary and are more likely to be promoted! If you want to fast-track your career and take advantage of all of the benefits of being a certified HR practitioner, sign up today!
