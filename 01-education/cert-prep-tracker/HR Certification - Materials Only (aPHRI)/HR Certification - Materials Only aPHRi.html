Features and Benefits

5 Study Manuals

These 5 study manuals are reflective of the "Bodies of Knowledge" that make up HR Certification Exams. All study manuals are updated and republished each year, to ensure that the materials you study are the up-to-date, and above all else, reflective of your upcoming exam. Print and soft copy included.

Quick-Testing Flash Cards

Course includes 100's of color-coded flashcards, featuring BuzzWords and definitions (reverse-side) to reinforce your ability to recall HR concepts by memory and by understanding.

Practice Exams

13 practice tests. The testing simulator very closely mimics the real resting scenario (timed, computer-based, layout and appearance) to ensure that there are no surprises when you take the real exam!
