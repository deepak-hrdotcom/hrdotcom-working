# Voice & Tone Guidelines

This document outlines the brand's voice and tone derived from the HR.com Manifesto Core Values.

## Brand Personality
Our brand and our marketing is a little unconventional and sassy (borderline snarky), but that’s only because we want everyone to know how innovative, fun, frugal, creative, and quirky we are. When communicating with clients (via phone, email, face-to-face, or marketing), it’s an opportunity to be unique, tap into core values, and showcase a memorable personality.

## 10 Core Values
1. **Focus on Happiness and Exceeding Expectations**: We WOW people with our service!
2. **Trust and Faith are the Pillars of our Communication**: We keep everyone in the circle of trust!
3. **Live and Love Passionately**: Passion is our middle name!
4. **Build a Positive Team and Family Spirit**: There’s no “I” in team!
5. **Learn Quick, Fail Fast, and Celebrate Success**: If at first you don’t succeed, try, try again!
6. **Embrace and Drive Change**: The best way to predict the future is to create it.
7. **Have Fun and Be Quirky**: We’re a little weird...but in a good way!
8. **Be Innovative, Creative, and Open-Minded**: We invent, experiment, grow, take risks, make mistakes and learn.
9. **Be Curious**: Let curiosity fuel results.
10. **Be Frugal and Spend Wisely**: We stay on budget like a boss!

## Commitments Sent to Audience
- Strive to design and offer comprehensive best-in-class learning experiences for the HR community based on expert guidance.
- Provide personalized guidance based on our HR industry expertise.
- Offer resources that enable HR to manage human capital to help organizations be compliant, progressive, and successful.
- Stay on the cutting edge of research and content.
- Never settle for ‘just ok’ or ‘good enough.’ We continually strive to help you be your best self. 
- Provide you with a community of like-minded individuals to share experiences.
- Give you the tools to mentor, coach, and help others to be the best they can be.

## Purpose, Mission, Vision & BHAG
**Purpose**: We help create engaged and inspired workforces.
**Mission**: We provide personalized guidance and tools to help HR professionals maximize their potential, navigate their careers, and transform HR teams to engage and inspire their workforce.
**Vision**: We see a world in which every HR professional has the education, skills, competencies, tools to create organizational value and inspired workforces.
**BHAG**: By 2040, we will impact 2.1 billion of the global workforce by empowering HR professionals in their careers, maximizing human potential.

## Rules for Copywriting
- Be engaging, conversational, yet highly professional and authoritative.
- Write with a unique, memorable flair—do not be boring or overly corporate.
- Emphasize community, continuous learning, and pushing boundaries.
- Support statements with data or research when applicable.
- Keep the overall structure clean, direct, and action-oriented.
