Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Prime HR memberships, recertification credits, and compliance resources.

An HR.com Prime Membership gives HR professionals comprehensive access to pre-approved HRCI and SHRM recertification credits, live and on-demand webcasts, federal and state labor law compliance tools powered by VirgilHR, customizable forms and handbooks, and member discounts on certification prep courses and in-person conferences.

To maintain your credential, HRCI (PHR, SPHR, GPHR) requires 60 recertification credits every three years (45 credits for aPHR). SHRM (SHRM-CP, SHRM-SCP) requires 60 Professional Development Credits (PDCs) every three years. Prime HR provides all the qualifying credits you need online.
