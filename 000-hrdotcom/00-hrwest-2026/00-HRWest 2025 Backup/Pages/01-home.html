THE "SUPER SEVEN" TRACKS

Everything HR Needs to Thrive in 2025

Being asked to do your job with less budget or personnel? You're not alone. HRWest expert content will help you move forward with clarity and confidence.

HR Strategy

HR must adapt new strategies and refine existing ones to meet the demands of today’s rapidly changing work world. Get 2025’s top insights on vision, goals, planning, implementation, and more in this track!

HR Tech

Technology’s importance for HR is spiking, especially with the rise of Generative AI. Learn the best practices, and insights about specific technologies that will help improve your day-to-day HR life and performance.

Talent

This track covers EVERYTHING from your company’s talent to developing your HR skills and career. Talent acquisition, talent management, retention, employee engagement, company culture, HR development — they’re all in here!

Legal and Compliance

Employment attorneys and other experts share critical compliance insights and updates to help you make sense of laws and regulations. Sessions in this highly popular track are often packed with attendees.

Health, Wellness, Benefits

Discover how to plan for a healthier and more productive workforce, and learn how to make your organization’s benefits and wellness offerings more modern and holistic to stand out from the competition.

Leadership

Learn what effective leadership looks like in the rapidly changing world of 2025, the latest development approaches, and more.

DEIB

It’s time for real progress! Get clarity about your role in DEIB, as well as on the challenges and opportunities for organizations in this important and controversial area.
