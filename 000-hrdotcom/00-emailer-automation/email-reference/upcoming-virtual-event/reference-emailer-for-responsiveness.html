Workplace Buzzwords

This Week’s Buzzword is ‘Hybrid Creep’

“Hybrid creep” describes the quiet rollback of hybrid work flexibility. It’s when companies subtly increase in-office expectations—adding extra days here and there—without officially changing policy. The result? A slow slide back to pre-pandemic office norms.

Where it came from:
The term was introduced by Owl Labs in its 2025 State of Hybrid Work Report, based on a survey of 2,000 U.S. workers. The company coined “hybrid creep” to capture how flexibility is shrinking—often unnoticed—across workplaces.

Why it matters:
It’s not just about office attendance. “Hybrid creep” signals a trust gap between employers and employees. It’s a reminder that flexibility isn’t a perk anymore. It’s part of how people define a healthy work culture.
