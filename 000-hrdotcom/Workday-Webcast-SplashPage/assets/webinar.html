Description

About Webcast

The way organizations attract, engage, and hire talent is undergoing a fundamental shift — and the pressure to modernize is greatest where it matters most: frontline worker industries. This session explores how a unified, AI-native talent acquisition strategy can eliminate the friction of fragmented systems and help your organization hire faster, smarter, and at scale.
Join us for a hands-on look at what a truly connected hiring experience looks like — from first touchpoint to first day.

In this session, you will:

Learn from a real-world transformation: hear directly from Workday customer OneDigital on how integrating Paradox reshaped their recruiting and hiring operations, and what that means for organizations facing similar challenges.
Understand how AI eliminates administrative burden: explore how Conversational Apply, intelligent scheduling automation, and AI-powered candidate prioritization through HiredScore AI for Recruiting free your recruiters and hiring managers to focus on high-value decisions — not manual screening.

Discover how a unified platform future-proofs your talent strategy: learn why native integration between Workday and Paradox matters: one platform, one data model, and AI that works with your existing workflows — not around them. Whether you're preparing for seasonal hiring surges or long-term workforce growth, see how the Workday platform scales with your competitive demands.
