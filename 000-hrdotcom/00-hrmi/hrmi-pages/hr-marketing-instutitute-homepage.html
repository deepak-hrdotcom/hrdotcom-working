What is the HR Marketing Institute?

This resource for marketing and selling to HR provides essential tricks, tools, and guidance for a community of marketers committed to inspiring each other to be their best and achieve amazing results and careers.  

 

What will I do as a member?

You’ll join other marketing professionals in face-to-face meetings and discussions in an online private community. You’ll also get access to industry research and best practices, compliance updates, benchmark data, case studies and other tools that will help you perform at your best.

What do I get with an HR Marketing Institute Membership?

  • Be part of an exclusive group of marketing professionals.
  • Collaborate online and in person.
  • Learn new techniques to market and grow your business.
  • Exclusive access to private, hosted forums on specific marketing issues, challenges and opportunities. Sample topics: AI, Ethics in Marketing, How to Make Your Marketing Messaging Attention-Grabbing, Lead Quality, What to Look For in an Agency/Partner, Vendor Evaluation and Selection Criteria.
  • Access to live and on-demand webcasts that provide valuable tips, how-tos and ”next” practices.
  • Participate in important research to help you benchmark your marketing against others in the human capital space.
  • Collaborate with peers at our live summit in an interactive conference format. (Choose the topics that appeal to you and send your entire marketing team.)
  • Access relevant case studies, marketing templates, engaging content and more.
