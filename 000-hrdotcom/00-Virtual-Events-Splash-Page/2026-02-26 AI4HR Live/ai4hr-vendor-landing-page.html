Gain HR Leads and Exposure with AI4HR Live Virtual Event Sponsorship

Sponsoring any of the editions of this virtual event will enable you to take a starring role in front of HR professionals as you showcase your AI insights, strategies, or solutions. You'll enjoy extended face time and all qualified leads, along with promotion to our network of more than 2 million HR executives.

What will the AI4HR virtual event reveal?

HR professionals are under pressure to leverage AI effectively — and they're looking for reliable guidance. This monthly event delivers practical insights on AI applications across recruitment, compliance, payroll, productivity, and emerging HR use cases. Attendees get fast-paced tips, real-world examples, and actionable strategies to stay current without information overload. It's where HR leaders come to understand what's working now and what's coming next in the rapidly changing world of AI.

The goals of this virtual event are to:

  • Equip HR professionals with practical AI knowledge — Deliver actionable insights and hands-on tips in an engaging way that makes AI4HR Live a can’t-miss virtual event each month.
  • Keep HR leaders ahead of critical trends — Provide timely updates on AI developments and emerging use cases in HR.
  • Connect innovative solution providers with HR decision-makers — Enable sponsors to showcase expertise and build relationships with active buyers.
 

Who Should Sponsor?

 

  • Suppliers that offer an AI-powered or AI-supported solution for organizations' human resources departments or workforces.
  • Any companies looking to showcase their expertise in the use of AI in HR.
  • Those organizations that wish to be seen as being a leader and innovator in AI.

Deliverables From This Collaborative Sponsorship

  • One-hour webcast presentation showcasing your expertise

  • Access to a real-time dashboard tracking and reporting on all leads — live and archived viewers

  • HR.com’s promotion of your virtual event and webcast

  • Full contact information for all virtual event registrants (including your webcast registrants) so you can create your own HR email list and fill your sales funnel

  • Attendees can earn HRCI/SHRM recertification credits

  • Ability to poll your webcast audience in real time, with live reports

  • An archive and MP4 of your webcast is downloadable for your clients, prospects, and HR.com members

  • Booth customization — let your creativity run wild!
