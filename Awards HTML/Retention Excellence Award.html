Nomination Deadline: January 8, 2027

Retention Excellence Award Information

Apply for this award if your organization has created strategies and programs that significantly reduce turnover and create a workplace where employees choose to stay and thrive.

Award Nomination Process

Entering is Free and Easy!

There is no charge to enter the awards. Simply fill out our brief questionnaire — it can be completed in just a few minutes. Our awards team will review your submission and let you know if any additional information is needed.

Once a nomination is submitted, you will receive a rating link to share with your network, peers, and colleagues to gather these required votes.

How Award Winners Are Determined

Award winners are selected through a combination of community support and expert evaluation. Important: Nominees must receive ratings to qualify as a winner.

A panel of judges will then carefully review each submission alongside its community ratings to select the final winner.

Contact awards@hr.com to learn more.
