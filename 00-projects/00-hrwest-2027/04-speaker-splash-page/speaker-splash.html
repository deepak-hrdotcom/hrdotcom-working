"Uncertainty is no longer temporary — it is structural. Artificial intelligence is reshaping core HR functions. Economic pressures are forcing organizations to do more with less. Workforce expectations are evolving faster than policies can adapt. Many HR professionals feel stuck reacting instead of leading."

How can HR rise above the fog? In this session, Adilen Valentina, SHRM-CP, introduces a practical leadership framework designed to help HR professionals move from reactive administration to strategic clarity.

Drawing on 15+ years of cross-sector HR and leadership development experience — and a municipal leadership initiative that produced 100% measurable competency improvement within 12 weeks — this session provides a clear, actionable path forward.

Key Learning Objectives & Takeaways

Clarify HR's Strategic Value in Disruption

Learn how to proactively articulate HR's high-leverage ROI during periods of organizational disruption and champion enterprise-wide change initiatives.

Deploy a Concise, Actionable Leadership Framework

Rather than offering high-level abstract theory alone, this session equips attendees with a concise leadership framework you can immediately apply inside your organization.

Reposition HR as Architects of Culture and Resilience

Leave with a renewed sense of direction and practical tools to reposition HR not as a back-office administration function, but as architects of culture, resilience, and long-term organizational performance.
