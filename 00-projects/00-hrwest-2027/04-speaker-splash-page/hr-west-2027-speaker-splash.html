Topic

Rising Above the Fog: A Strategic Leadership Framework for Modern HR

Jun 10, 2026 2:40 pm - 3:10 pm PT

Uncertainty is no longer temporary — it is structural. Artificial intelligence is reshaping core HR functions. Economic pressures are forcing organizations to do more with less. Workforce expectations are evolving faster than policies can adapt. Many HR professionals feel stuck reacting instead of leading.

How can HR rise above the fog? In this session, Adilen Valentina, SHRM-CP, introduces a practical leadership framework designed to help HR professionals move from reactive administration to strategic clarity. Drawing on 15+ years of cross-sector HR and leadership development experience — and a municipal leadership initiative that produced 100% measurable competency improvement within 12 weeks — this session provides a clear, actionable path forward.

Participants will explore how to:

  • Clarify HR’s strategic value in times of disruption and lead change.

Rather than offering theory alone, this session equips attendees with a concise leadership framework they can immediately apply inside their organizations. Attendees will leave with a renewed sense of direction — and practical tools to reposition HR not as a back-office function, but as architects of culture, resilience, and long-term organizational performance.
