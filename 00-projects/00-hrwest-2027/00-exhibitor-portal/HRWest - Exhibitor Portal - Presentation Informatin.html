Roundtable Discussion

As a roundtable discussion leader, you’ll guide an engaging and dynamic session designed to spark meaningful conversations. Over two separate 30-minute sessions, you’ll facilitate discussions with two distinct groups of attendees, allowing for organic interaction and the exchange of valuable ideas. With no formal presentations or AV components, your role is to bring your expertise and insights to the table, fostering an inclusive, conversational atmosphere.

Session Presentation

Sessions are scheduled for 30 minutes, including Q&A time. There are four concurrent sessions on both days of the conference. Each session typically sees between 100 and 125 attendees. There will be five minutes between sessions to check AV and connect with your Session Host. 

Computer and AV Tech 

The session rooms will not be equipped with a laptop, so please bring your own. We still ask that you send us a copy of your presentation by March 15. You never know when tech difficulties may arise, and we want to support you with both a Plan A and a Plan B.

Session Hosts

We will have a volunteer session host in each session room to introduce you before your presentation and provide assistance throughout your session. Kindly send us your preferred introduction, limited to 100 words by March 15 to your assigned Account Partner. Feel free to have fun with this and share something that our audience would not discover on your LinkedIn profile. 

Session Presentation Information

Feel free to use the linked presentation template (PPT) for your session and provide your presentation in either PPT or PDF format by March 15 to your assigned Account Partner. Please do not submit a presentation in Google Slides format. All video should be embedded into the presentation file. If HR.com is not at liberty to share the Speaker’s presentation materials with HRWest 2027 attendees post-event, please notify your assigned Account Partner by March 1, 2027.
