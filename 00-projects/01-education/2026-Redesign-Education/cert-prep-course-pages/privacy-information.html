3. What are cookies and how does HR.com use them?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer's hard drive - to help track your usage of our site. Cookies don't include personal information about you, but they are unique to each user, which allows HR.com computers to distinguish between users and can save you time when you return to a part of the site and repeat an activity. Cookies placed on your computer by third parties by or through the Site, if any, are not the responsibility of HR.com and are not subject to the Terms and Conditions (to view our Terms and Conditions, click here).

While HR.com does not require you to use cookies, keep in mind that certain services will not function properly if you set your browser to refuse all cookies. To help serve you better, HR.com generally uses cookies to:

  • Identify return visitors. Cookies let us remember your web browser so we can provide personalized member services. Cookies also allow us to identify HR.com members who are returning to the site.
  • Display advertisements. HR.com uses an outside ad company to display HR.com-approved ads on our website and other websites. While we use cookies on other parts of our website(s), the cookies received with banner ads are collected by our ad company. These cookies allow HR.com to manage the delivery of ads.
