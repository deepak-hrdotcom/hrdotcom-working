Before 2027 begins, prepare your team behind the plan.

Every leadership team will face hard calls in 2027. Whether your strategy holds depends on the team that must carry it: whether they trust each other enough to argue, commit once the argument is over, and hold each other to it when the quarter gets difficult.

That has been Patrick Lencioni’s subject for nearly 30 years. This December he’ll join us live to walk through the ideas CEOs return to most, from The Five Dysfunctions of a Team and The Advantage to The Ideal Team Player and The 6 Types of Working Genius. He’ll take your questions throughout.

What you and your team will take into 2027

Most planning cycles spend weeks on the numbers and one afternoon on the people expected to deliver them. The result is a strong plan in the hands of a leadership group that can’t agree on what matters most, doesn’t know how to disagree productively, and ends up re-arguing the same decisions in March.

Pat flips that script. This live session is built for CEOs and senior executives who want their teams ready for a demanding year.

A shared understanding of how your team performs under pressure.

Using the Five Dysfunctions model, you'll see where trust, healthy conflict, commitment, accountability and focus on results tend to give way when the stakes rise, and what to do about it early.

Clarity that holds all the way through the company.

The Advantage is built on a small set of questions every leadership team has to answer together, from why the company exists to what matters most right now. Teams that can't answer them the same way can't expect anyone else to.

Better people decisions.

When headcount is on the table, the humble, hungry and smart framework from The Ideal Team Player gives you a common language for who to hire, who to develop and who to move on from.

The right people on the right work.

Working Genius shows where each member of your team does their best work and where they get worn down, which matters when you're deciding who owns which parts of the plan.

About Patrick Lencioni

Few people have shaped how leadership teams work together more than Patrick Lencioni. The Five Dysfunctions of a Team, first published in 2002, is still among the most widely read business books of the past quarter century, and its model has become shorthand in executive teams around the world.

Pat is the founder and president of The Table Group and a pioneer of the organizational health movement. He’s the author of 13 books, which have sold more than 10 million copies in over 30 languages. His books are known for a distinctive approach: short, readable leadership fables that make complicated team dynamics easy to recognize in your own company. Before founding The Table Group in 1997, he worked at Bain & Company, Oracle and Sybase.

The Wall Street Journal has called him “one of the most in demand speakers in America.” He has worked with executive teams at companies of every size and in every sector, and he hosts the podcast At the Table with Patrick Lencioni. He’s been a returning voice at Chief Executive events, including our Leadership Conference.
